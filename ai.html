Channel decomposition

Domestic: gains from domestic productivity with expenditure shares and network fixed; includes domestic gains travelling through international value chains back to home consumers.

Foreign: same for foreign productivity improvements (cheaper final and intermediate imports, direct + upstream).

Competitiveness: real-income effect of the induced change in the global production network; sub-term 1 (local-global mismatch) = discrepancy between local and global changes in factor demands; sub-term 2 = income change from factor reallocation. Negative when a country loses global market share (e.g., non-adopters in B1); slightly positive for fast adopters [pp. 34–39].

These three keys are read by NO code. The numeraire is hard-coded at src/oecd_ai/model/equilibrium.py:116 and renormalises on every evaluate(); closure.alt_imbalance names two ALT hooks that were specified but never implemented (build_model has no such parameter); solver.numeraire_renorm is unreferenced. Leaving them dead and DECLARED is fine. The danger is a later refactor that 'honours the config' and thereby moves the numeraire of a validated model while appearing to be a no-op.
