LAB EXTENSIONINDEPENDENT REIMPLEMENTATION
This is an accounting exposure calculation, not a forecast. It answers: given the production relationships observed in Singapore's 2023 Input-Output Tables, how much gross output, value added and compensation of employees was associated with the final demand you have shocked? It assumes fixed input proportions, no price responses, no substitution between inputs or suppliers, no capacity constraints, no income-induced consumption, and no dynamics. Results are in 2023 current prices.
Full accounting disclosure

Mandatory disclosures (specification section 12.5, all seven, in spec order)

  • 1. Reference year and releaseSingStat Supply, Use and Input-Output Tables, reference year 2023, details classification (n = 107), dataLastUpdated 25/03/2026. Annual tables such as this one are not benchmark tables (pitfall P9): only 2010 and 2015 are.
  • 2. Prices and unitsCurrent prices, S$ million, 2023 basis. Not adjusted for inflation and never annualised or extrapolated.
  • 3. Price basis of the shockShock stated at basic prices; no purchasers'-to-basic conversion applied. The published cells it is applied to are basic-price cells (T3/T4/T5). Base cells: T3 product x final-demand block (domestic use at basic prices). Magnitude applied as a proportional change to each selected cell.
  • 4. Domestic share applied and direct import leakages = 1 by construction: the shock is applied to the T3 column, which is final demand for domestically produced output only, so the split has already been performed by the publisher and no direct import leakage arises. Direct import leakage removed from the domestic propagation: 0.0000 S$ million.
  • 5. Export split usedThe shock touches exports. Split used: the DOMESTIC-exports vector, T3/T5's 'Exports of goods & services' column, which is domestic exports despite its label (spec Q6 and pitfall P5; verified bit-exact against T14 column 2.1). T4's export column is 'Re-exports of goods & services' and is untouched by this shock. Pitfall P3: 2023 total exports 1,258,332.55 versus domestic exports 973,453.01 S$ million; pitfall P4: the import content of domestic exports is 54.0 percent economy-wide (054 Wholesale trade 46.0 percent; 057 Water transport 71.5 percent; 026 Semiconductor devices, electronic components and boards 62.0 percent), so the gross-output figure above is not the domestic stake.
  • 6. Model classModel class: open static Leontief, fixed coefficients, no induced consumption. Fixed input proportions, no price responses, no substitution between inputs or suppliers, no capacity constraints, no income-induced consumption, no dynamics. Imports are non-competing (a separate import use table), so Θ is the domestic Leontief inverse.
  • 7. Value added as a share of gross outputValue added is 28.67 percent of the gross-output exposure of this shock. Economy-wide 2023: 651,551.19 / 1,897,859.58 = 34.3 percent. Gross output is not value added; the wedge is import content and intermediate double counting.
