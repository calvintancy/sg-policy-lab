what changedConverts a 'productivity' shock into the 'productivity' loading the kernel accepts. In: cumulative log change in value-added TFP (log points, dimensionless). Out: gross-output productivity hat A-hat (ratio; 1.0 = no change). Formula: Per World Bank country c and WB-21 sector J: g_J = sum_{i in J} VA_i * g_i / sum_{i in J} VA_i (VA-weighted value-added TFP log change); alpha_J = sum_{i in J} alpha_i * OUT_i / sum_{i in J} OUT_i (gross-output-weighted primary-factor share, alpha = (VA + TLS) / OUT); hat_gross_equiv = exp(alpha_J * g_J). The unconverted value-added loading hat_va = exp(g_J) is retained as the upper bound and is never fed to the kernel as if it were a gross-output hat.
why defensibleThe World Bank kernel shifts unit cost through a gross-output productivity hat. Feeding it a value-added TFP hat directly overstates the shock by roughly 1/alpha (about a factor of two at typical primary shares), which is the error this bridge exists to make impossible. Marking the capability 'translated' rather than 'native' forces the named conversion to be recorded on the scenario before any number is produced.
