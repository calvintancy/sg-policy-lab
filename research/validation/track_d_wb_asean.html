7. Domain and support

Shock-semantics warning

The scenario is NOT convergence to Korea's level. It re-runs the reference country's realised 1995->2022 trade-cost change (Head-Ries symmetric index) starting from the target's 2022 base. That is why the magnitudes are so large (THA +41% real income from trade costs alone). Any interface must state this, or a policymaker will read it as a level target.
